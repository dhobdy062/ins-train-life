📚 Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome, Trainer! This page helps you configure the platform for your team. Use these FAQs to understand how everything works, then customize the objections, rebuttals, and difficulty levels for your specific market.

For Trainees

How do difficulty levels work? ⋯

D1: Prospect remembers form, low resistance – builds confidence

D2: Skeptical, doesn't remember you – teaches credibility-building

D3: Family/timing concerns – teaches collaborative selling

D4: False confidence (already covered) – teaches needs analysis

D5: Brick wall – tests mastery of all techniques

What happens when I get an objection? ⋯

The voice agent presents an objection. You have 30 seconds to respond with an appropriate rebuttal. The system scores your response (0-100 points) and the prospect reacts based on your score. You'll see immediate feedback on whether you used the right strategy.

How am I scored? ⋯

Rebuttal Type Accuracy (40%): Did you use the correct strategy?

Execution Quality (30%): Tone, specificity, empathy, clarity

Timing (15%): Responded within 30 seconds?

Prospect Response (15%): Did it move toward appointment-setting?

For Trainers

How do I track my agents' progress? ⋯

Go to the Agent Management tab. You'll see each agent's call history, average score by difficulty level, weak areas (objection types they struggle with), and recommendations for next training focus.

Can I customize the objections my agents see? ⋯

Yes! Go to the Objection Library tab. You can edit wording, add new objections, map them to rebuttal types, and adjust frequency. This allows you to tailor training to your specific market and product.

When should I move an agent to the next difficulty level? ⋯

Look for: Average score >75% on current level for 10+ calls, consistent success with all objection types, zero "hard stops" (prospects asking to be removed from list). You can set auto-progression rules or manually move agents up.

What's a "hard stop"? ⋯

When a prospect explicitly asks to be removed from your list, hangs up in frustration, or says "Stop calling"—it's logged as a hard stop. The agent scores 0 points on that objection. Too many hard stops indicate tone issues that need coaching.

What metrics should I track? ⋯

Key KPIs: Appointment Set Rate (%), Average Rebuttal Score, Objection Success Rate by type, Level Progression speed (D1→D5), Hard Stop Rate (should be <5%)
